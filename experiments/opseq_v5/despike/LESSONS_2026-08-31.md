## 1. Needle-removal recap (why v22 works, one paragraph)

A per-vertex "projects outside GT" test (v21, pure B) can only see the needle's
TIP (~195 verts, 15–281 px/view) because the trained needle's shaft lies buried
inside the body silhouette in all 6 training views. Amputating the tip leaves
the shaft → regrows. v22 separates SEED (2D GT-mismatch = tip escape) from
EXTENT (3D thickness = whole shaft) and binds them by connected-component
propagation: condemn a thin component iff it contains an escape seed. Needle
root-and-all removed; ear (thin, no seed) spared. train6 0.9813 / ho16 0.9568 /
hair 9100 / maxblob 721 — best on every metric.

**Lesson**: when a defect is only *partially* observable in the supervision
views, use the observable part as a seed and a *geometric invariant of the
defect* (here: thinness) to define the removal extent. Per-vertex evidence
gating fails; component-level propagation succeeds.

## 2. Depth is real (`depth360.py`)

GT-vs-v22 depth 360° turntable (NDC-z, turbo, shared range) confirms the
result carries correct near/far structure, not just silhouette. The depth loss
(W_DEPTH=0.35 + phase-in) does its job — but see §4: it is also the main
source of surface noise.

## 3. Generalization to other shapes (`run_v22.py`)

Same pipeline, zero retuning:

| shape | train6 | ho16 | hair_px | verdict |
|-------|--------|------|---------|---------|
| cow | 0.9813 | 0.9568 | 9100 | reference |
| bunny | 0.9862 | 0.9596 | 20598 | good — near-spherical topology suits icosphere init |
| armadillo | 0.9793 | 0.9149 | 33307 | torso only — splayed thin limbs/claws never grow |

**Lesson**: the pipeline transfers as-is to blob-like genus-0 shapes. The
armadillo failure is NOT a discriminator problem — silhouette+depth vertex
gradients cannot *grow* long thin protrusions from a sphere. The GREEN
mismatch (GT present, pred missing) marks exactly where growth is needed; the
error-driven TopMod extrude injection (commit `ff0a6ab`) is the matching tool.
RED mismatch → delete needles (solved, v22); GREEN mismatch → grow limbs/ears
(open, extrude). The two are symmetric halves of one framework.

Gotcha: `cow_v13.heldout_exam` hardcodes cow.obj — `run_v22.py` carries its own
generic exam. Available GT models in the pymeshlab sample dir: cow, bunny,
armadillo, fandisk, airplane, cube.

## 4. Surface smoothness (`taubin_test.py`, `cow_v23.py`)

Diagnosis of the bumpy surface: NO surface-smoothness term exists in the
recipe (uniform Laplacian W_LAP=0.1 is weak; fold penalty 0.02 only fights
antiparallel pairs). Per-pixel depth L1 (W_DEPTH=0.35) actively injects
high-frequency facet noise on camera-facing regions.

Experiments (judge smoothness with SHADED renders — silhouettes hide it):

- **Post-hoc Taubin** (λ=0.5, μ=-0.53): visually smooth but blind to the
  silhouette — costs 2.5–4.4 IoU points (5 it: 0.9560, 10 it: 0.9469,
  20 it: 0.9375 vs raw 0.9813). Too expensive as a standalone fix.
- **In-training normal consistency** (v23: `W_NC·mean(1−n_i·n_j)` over
  adjacent face pairs, injected by patching edge_length_loss with weight
  W_NC/W_EDGE, gated to V>2000): at **W_NC=0.10 visually reaches DMesh-level
  smoothness** but over-flattens: train6 0.9707, ho16 0.9146, hair 27125 —
  flattening also disturbs the needle-removal dynamics.

**Lesson**: smoothing must happen *during* training (so silhouette+depth keep
it on-target), but W_NC=0.10 is too strong. Next: W_NC∈{0.03,0.05}, settle
phase only, and/or robustify the depth term (the noise source). The clean
injection trick (patch a per-step loss hook, scale by W_NC/W_EDGE for an
independent effective weight) is reusable.

## 5. DMesh study (arXiv 2404.13445, NeurIPS 2024)

How DMesh actually works: Weighted Delaunay Triangulation + differentiable
**face-existence probability**; multi-view mode renders face probability as
opacity through a differentiable renderer with image L1. Regularizers:
L_weight (simplify via dual power diagram), L_real (neighbor coherence →
removes holes/inner junk), L_qual (expected aspect ratio → kills slivers).
**No Laplacian, no normal consistency, no coarse-to-fine.**

**Key insight**: DMesh's clean look comes from *representation* (WDT yields
well-shaped triangles) + *triangle quality regularization*, not from any
smoothing loss. Differentiable rendering is one supervision channel (images),
not the essence — point-cloud mode uses none.

### TopMod vs DMesh positioning

TopMod advantages over DMesh output: constructive 2-manifold guarantee (DMesh
admits non-manifold edges as a limitation), audited genus/χ, **editable
operator programs** (mesh = replayable op sequence, not a static triangle
soup), structured coarse-to-fine base, n-gon support. DMesh gives a result;
TopMod gives a process.

### Combination points (ranked)

1. **Hybrid pipeline (short-term, highest value)**: DMesh for topology genesis
   (grow armadillo limbs) → DLFL conversion + manifold repair (TopMod as
   cleanup layer) → v22 de-needle + operator refinement (TopMod as edit
   layer). Directly addresses the §3 armadillo failure.
2. **Differentiable operator-existence probability (long-term, paper-grade)**:
   port DMesh's core trick — soften discrete choices — to TopMod operator
   decisions (where to extrude / which edge to collapse), trained by render
   loss, replacing the hand-crafted error-voting trigger.
3. **L_qual expected-aspect-ratio** form is directly portable (upgrade of our
   sliver_pen).
4. Our escape-seed component propagation is already a discrete cousin of
   L_real neighbor coherence.
